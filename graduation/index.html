How Grad House works.

Choose a package to see details.
Booking process
  1. Pick the package that fits your session.
  2. Search your university / shoot location, choose a date and load live times.
  3. Choose the time you want and complete the required booking details.
  4. Continue to WhatsApp to confirm availability or request a final adjustment.
Cancel / move process
  • Send the change on WhatsApp with your package, date and location.
  • Reschedules move to the next suitable available slot.
  • Late cancellations or no-shows may forfeit the agreed booking fee / held slot.
  • Weather or campus issues can be moved to the next practical opening.
